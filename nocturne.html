03 / Gameplay → Combat

A short window.

Punches need to connect when the player expects them to. The footage is the starting point; the hit detection is what makes it possible.

Technical focus / Raycasts

I implemented real-time raycast hit detection to register punches within short hitbox durations.

The challenge is timing: a brief attack window still needs to produce a clear, satisfying connection.

05 / System → GameplayNetwork replication

Local response.
Shared impact.

An attack begins on one machine. Its feedback needs to reach the others.

I built client-server communication that initiates attacks locally, validates them on the server and replicates their VFX when accepted.

The goal: reduce perceived delay while keeping the encounter visually consistent.

07 / TakeawaysResponsiveness is a systems problem

Combat feel reaches
across the network.

Hit timing and replication have to be designed together.

The work brought raycast detection, client-side attack initiation and server validation into one combat flow. It made the relationship between local responsiveness and shared feedback central to the design.

The technical focus: short hit windows, clear validation boundaries and consistent visual feedback.
